# Donby Agent Changelog — v1.3.29 to v1.3.34

> Historical shard covering the 4K Remux seek smoothness, multi-MiB Range streaming, prewarm, and Dock inode stabilization cycle.

## 212d4be | 2026-11-21 | v1.3.34 | Instant-start, uncached 4K seek smoothness & prewarm pipelines
- **Scope**: `turbo-network`, `kernel-cpp`, `metal-renderer`, `app-player-ui`, `storage-core`
- **Root Cause / Goal**: Reduce cold startup and uncached 4K seek jitter, isolate test storage paths, and prewarm playback metadata/tracks.
- **Changes**:
  - `Sources/DonbyCore/DonbyStoragePaths.swift`: Added centralized storage path abstraction with `DONBY_HOME` override for isolated test runs.
  - `Sources/DonbyApp/PlayerView.swift` (`computePreOpenTrackHints`, `applySubtitleOptionOnStartup`, `prefetchNextEpisodeIfNearEnd`): Pre-computed track hints before `dk_player_open_with_tracks` and added near-EOF next-episode prefetching.
  - `Sources/DonbyKernelC/dk_engine.cpp` (`dk_required_audio_preroll_samples`): Made audio preroll adaptive to rebuffer state.
  - `Sources/DonbyNetwork/TurboCacheAndDownloadEngine.swift`, `EmbyClient.swift`, `UHDStreamClient.swift`, `OfflineMediaLibrary.swift`: Added prewarm pipelines and routed storage via `DonbyStoragePaths`.
- **Invariants**: All disk cache/storage callers must route through `DonbyStoragePaths`.

## 57705a2 | 2026-11-20 | v1.3.33 | Preserve `/Applications/Donby.app` inode in-place & auto-detach residual DMGs
- **Scope**: `build-release`, `app-player-ui`, `turbo-network`
- **Root Cause / Goal**: Replacing `/Applications/Donby.app` via `rm -rf` changed its directory inode, causing macOS Dock to show question marks or duplicate Donby icons; residual mounted DMGs also polluted LaunchServices.
- **Changes**:
  - `build_app.sh` & `Sources/DonbyNetwork/AppUpdateManager.swift`: Replaced `Contents/` in-place to preserve top-level `.app` inode.
  - `Sources/DonbyApp/DonbyApp.swift` (`detachVolume`): Auto-detached residual mounted Donby DMG volumes on app startup.
- **Invariants**: Never `rm -rf /Applications/Donby.app` during install/update; replace `Contents/` in-place.

## d61c2c3 | 2026-11-20 | v1.3.32 | Multi-MiB continuous Range streaming for 1.3s-TTFB CDN & auto-rebuffer cushion
- **Scope**: `turbo-network`, `kernel-cpp`
- **Root Cause / Goal**: Fetching individual 512 KiB chunks over a `1.3s`-TTFB CDN incurred repeated RTT penalties; audio underruns caused micro-stutters instead of clean cushion recovery.
- **Changes**:
  - `Sources/DonbyNetwork/TurboCacheAndDownloadEngine.swift`: Upgraded to multi-MiB continuous HTTP Range streams (`covers(_ idx:)`) that populate multiple 512 KiB chunks per request.
  - `Sources/DonbyKernelC/dk_engine.cpp`: Added automatic rebuffer cushion upon audio underrun.

## 8a307f6 | 2026-11-20 | v1.3.31 | Eliminate 4K Remux post-seek 5-way bandwidth splitting, straggler HOL stall & 85ms AudioQueue underrun
- **Scope**: `turbo-network`, `kernel-cpp`, `app-player-ui`
- **Root Cause / Goal**: Immediately launching 5 equal-priority requests after a seek split bandwidth 5 ways and created head-of-line stalls on the urgent playhead chunk; AudioQueue started with only ~85ms of audio and immediately underran.
- **Changes**:
  - `Sources/DonbyNetwork/TurboCacheAndDownloadEngine.swift`: Prioritized playhead stream after seek and mitigated straggler HOL stalls.
  - `Sources/DonbyKernelC/dk_engine.cpp` (`dk_is_seeking`, `dk_try_start_audio_queue_if_ready`): Gated AudioQueue startup behind minimum buffered audio cushion and clean seek state.

## a0c6d86 | 2026-11-20 | v1.3.30 | 30GB seek jitter fix, anti-sparse cache compaction, persistent search & geometric vector icons
- **Scope**: `turbo-network`, `kernel-cpp`, `app-player-ui`
- **Root Cause / Goal**: Seeking deep into 30 GiB+ files created sparse `media.bin` files (huge apparent disk allocation) and post-seek frame jitter; search history was ephemeral.
- **Changes**:
  - `Sources/DonbyNetwork/TurboCacheAndDownloadEngine.swift`: Implemented anti-sparse spill chunk storage (`chunks/<idx>.chk`) + sequential folding compaction into `media.bin`.
  - `Sources/DonbyKernelC/dk_engine.cpp` & `donby_kernel.h`: Stabilized 30GB seek flush and decode queue synchronization.
  - `Sources/DonbyApp/AppState.swift` & `Views/GlobalSearchView.swift`: Persisted search history (`recordSearchHistory`, `removeSearchHistoryItem`, `clearSearchHistory`).
  - `Sources/DonbyApp/Components/BrandIconsAndToolbar.swift`: Added custom geometric vector icons (`DownloadVectorIcon`, `GridWallVectorIcon`, `FolderVectorIcon`, `FinderRevealVectorIcon`, `SortArrowsVectorIcon`, `PlayVectorIcon`).

## a5fd0bf | 2026-11-20 | v1.3.29 | Fix unbuffered seek freeze, 429 recovery & multi-MiB batch downloads
- **Scope**: `turbo-network`, `kernel-cpp`, `metal-renderer`, `app-player-ui`
- **Root Cause / Goal**: Unbuffered seeks could deadlock/freeze video rendering; HTTP 429 responses lacked graceful concurrency backoff; offline downloads were slow with small per-chunk requests.
- **Changes**:
  - `Sources/DonbyNetwork/TurboCacheAndDownloadEngine.swift`: Added HTTP 429 backoff/recovery and multi-MiB batched download requests.
  - `Sources/DonbyKernelC/dk_engine.cpp` & `Sources/DonbyMetalRenderer/DKMetalVideoView.swift` & `PlayerView.swift`: Fixed unbuffered seek freeze and frame presentation handoff.
