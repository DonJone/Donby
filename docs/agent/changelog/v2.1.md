# Changelog Shard: v2.1.x

## v2.1.4 | 2026-11-28 | Pre-release | Frontend Performance Overhaul (PERF-01..PERF-17)
- **Scope**: `DonbyApp` (`AppState*`, `MainWindowView`, `EmbyMainView`, `DetailView*`, `LogViewerSheet`, `Components/*`, `Views/*`, `Player/*`, `Modules/UHD/UHDDanmuViews`), `DonbyMetalRenderer` (`DKMetalLayerView*`, `DKMetalShadersAndPipeline`, `DKMetalVideoView`), `DonbyNetwork` (`DonbyDownloadManager`), `DonbyCore` (`AppUpdatePolicies`), `Tests/DonbyCoreTests` (`DonbyCoreTests`), `build_app.sh`, `scripts/package_dmg.sh`, `docs/agent`
- **Root Cause / Goal**: Eliminate SwiftUI body re-evaluation storms during playback/downloads/toast updates, off-load synchronous startup disk reads, remove redundant Metal/ImageIO/CoreText work, and virtualize heavy list surfaces (`PERF-01`..`PERF-17`).
- **Changes**:
  - **Home, State & Download Index Isolation (`PERF-01`, `PERF-02`, `PERF-06`, `PERF-09`, `PERF-11`)**: Added `AppState+PerformanceStores.swift` (`DonbyToastStore`, `DonbyToastOverlayView`, `downloadStatusIndex` suppression during `activePlayback`), `cachedHomeShelves`/`homeRevision` fast-path in `NativeEmbyHomeView` with visible-cell-only badge updates, `isPingingLines`/`isSyncingDomains` guards, async `Task.detached` startup hydration (`iptvChannels`, `watchHistory`, `cachedSeriesNames`, `urlMediaItems`) + `ServerHomeCacheStore` async preload/fallback, conditional off-MainActor `scanOfflineLibrary`, scoped `DetailView` `observedDownloadIndex` + completion-only `refreshOfflineAndCacheSnapshots`, and integer-percent quantization + `playableBackingFileCache` in `DonbyDownloadManager.rebuildStatusIndex`.
  - **Player HUD Telemetry & Metal Renderer (`PERF-03`, `PERF-04`, `PERF-08`, `PERF-15`, `PERF-16`)**: Introduced `PlayerHUDTelemetry` with quantized updates and leaf observers (`PlayerTopSpeedView`, `PlayerSpecBadgeHostView`, `PlayerBottomControlBarView`, `PlayerBufferingOverlayHostView`, `PlayerMediaInfoDrawerContentView`), live-preview `draftScale`/`draftVerticalPos` in `PlayerSubtitleStyleDrawerView`, `LazyVStack` in `.playlist`, static `ISO8601DateFormatter`, `Coordinator` cached viewport metrics + reusable `SubtitleSyncBox` + `subtitleLiftDirty` fast-path + `DKMetalLayerView` size-diff guards, and slimmed `DKToneMapConstants` `[[buffer(1)]]` + 16-byte `DKSubtitleFragmentUniforms` while preserving 3088-byte `DKGpuColorUniformsMSL`.
  - **Artwork, Liquid Glass & Virtualized Views (`PERF-05`, `PERF-07`, `PERF-10`, `PERF-12`, `PERF-13`, `PERF-14`, `PERF-17`)**: Added eager ImageIO decode + `normalizeToDisplaySpace` fast-return + `nil` `urlCache`/`.reloadIgnoringLocalCacheData`, `DonbyVisualEffectView` `updateNSView` guards + `scaleEffect` replacing `GeometryReader`, `LogViewerSheet` debounced `cachedFilteredEntries` (150ms), `LazyVStack` in `DownloadsView` and `UHDDanmuPickerSheet`, memoized filtered collections in `RecordsView`/`IPTVModuleView`/`FilesModuleView`, `RecordArtworkThumbnailView` `isLightMode` prop, `NativeCardLayerCache` caps (`pillCache 512`, `iconCache 256`, `symbolCGCache 256`), and `DetailView+SeasonsAndEpisodes` border styling replacing dynamic shadow.
- **Invariants & Pitfalls**:
  - Keep `PlayerView.body` and `DKMetalVideoView` free of `PlayerHUDTelemetry` reads; preserve 3088-byte `DKGpuColorUniformsMSL` layout; keep `Tests/DonbyCoreTests/DonbyCoreTests.swift` and all source files `<= 800L`.
- **Verification**:
  - `DONBY_TEST_MUTED=1 DONBY_HOME=$(mktemp -d) ./scripts/dev.sh test`, `DONBY_PURE=1` build & test, and `python3 scripts/check_agent_docs.py --staged` PASS.

## v2.1.3 | 2026-11-27 | Pre-release | Intel Older Mac & x86_64 Hackintosh Support (macOS 13+ Floor, Discrete GPU Metal, VT Fallback & Dual-Arch CI)
- **Scope**: `Package.swift`, `DonbyCore` (`AppUpdatePolicies`, `ServerCredentialStore`), `DonbyNetwork` (`AppUpdateManager`), `DonbyMetalRenderer` (`DKMetalLayerView*`, `DKMetalShadersAndPipeline`), `DonbyKernelC` (`dk_ffi_video.c`), `KernelRust` (`video`, `lifecycle`, `abi`), `DonbyApp` (macOS 13 shims + `SettingsView+About`), `DonbyUHDModule` (`UHDAppExtensionPlugin`), `Tests/DonbyCoreTests` (`PlatformAndArchitectureTests`), build/release scripts, `.github/workflows/build-dmg.yml`, `README.md`, `docs/agent`
- **Root Cause / Goal**: Enable native, reliable playback, Metal rendering, auto-updates, and CI/CD packaging for older Intel Macs (2017+ Ventura, dual-GPU MacBook Pros, Xeon iMac Pro/Mac Pro, OCLP Macs) and `x86_64` Hackintoshes (discrete AMD GPUs, headless iGPUs, broken VDA/VT, missing `IOPlatformUUID`, Broadcom Wi-Fi pinned to macOS 13) ([ADR-006](../decisions/006-intel-x64-and-hackintosh-support.md)).
- **Changes**:
  - **macOS 13.0 (Ventura) Floor & SwiftUI Shims (`Package.swift`, `DonbyThemeControls.swift`, `DonbyApp/*`, `build_app.sh`, `build_kernel.sh`)**: Lowered minimum OS target to `13.0`, added `donbyFocusEffectDisabled()` / `donbyOnChange(of:perform:)` / `DonbyPlayerKeyPressModifier`, and made `Package.swift` Homebrew prefix resolution arch-aware (`DONBY_ARCH` / `DONBY_DEPS_PREFIX` / `/usr/local`).
  - **Discrete AMD & Intel iGPU Metal Fixes (`DKMetalLayerView.swift`, `DKMetalLayerView+Rendering.swift`, `DKMetalShadersAndPipeline.swift`)**: Switched 2D `MTLTexture` allocations from hardcoded `.shared` to `dev.hasUnifiedMemory ? .shared : .managed` with `MTLBlitCommandEncoder.synchronize(resource:)` before offscreen `getBytes`, added `CGDirectDisplayCopyCurrentMetalDevice` display-driven GPU selection and live screen/GPU reconfiguration, and negotiated `.bgra8Unorm_srgb` on low-power SDR `x86_64` iGPUs or legacy OCLP Metal shims.
  - **VideoToolbox Fallback & `x86_64` Software Decode (`KernelRust/src/{video,lifecycle,abi}.rs`, `dk_ffi_video.c`)**: Bypassed VT on `__x86_64__` for AV1 and Hi10p H.264, triggered `fallback_video_to_software()` on `receive_frame < 0` and null `CVPixelBuffer`s with `signal_seek_request` keyframe resync, raised software decode thread clamp to `12`, and added `-O3` auto-vectorizable direct `YUV420P -> NV12` and `YUV420P10LE -> P010` packers.
  - **Hackintosh Vault Fallback & Strict Multi-Arch Updater (`ServerCredentialStore.swift`, `AppUpdatePolicies.swift`, `AppUpdateManager.swift`, `SettingsView+About.swift`)**: Added `IOPlatformSerialNumber` fallback when `IOPlatformUUID` is absent, Rosetta-aware `DonbyAppInfo.preferredUpdateArchitecture`, strict `classifyAssetArchitecture` matching (never crossing `arm64`/`x86_64`), multi-arch `platforms` `latest.json` parsing, and pre-replace Mach-O slice validation (`machOBinarySupportsArchitecture`).
  - **Dual-Arch Build Scripts & GitHub Actions (`build_kernel.sh`, `build_app.sh`, `bundle_dylibs.py`, `package_dmg.sh`, `release_local.sh`, `.github/workflows/build-dmg.yml`)**: Added `DONBY_ARCH` (`arm64` / `x86_64` / `universal`) support and updated CI to build missing `arm64` (`macos-15`) and `x86_64` (`macos-15-intel`) artifacts and merge `SHA256SUMS.txt` + `latest.json` into the release.
- **Invariants & Pitfalls**:
  - Never pass `MTLStorageMode.shared` to 2D `MTLTextureDescriptor` when `device.hasUnifiedMemory == false`; never select an opposite-architecture update asset; keep all source files `<= 800L`.
- **Verification**:
  - `./scripts/dev.sh test` and `DONBY_PURE=1 ./scripts/dev.sh build && DONBY_PURE=1 ./scripts/dev.sh test`: Rust kernel (`13/13`), Swift `DonbyCoreTests` + `PlatformAndArchitectureTests`, and `check_agent_docs.py` PASS.
  - `./scripts/release_local.sh 2.1.3 --prerelease`: packaged `arm64` DMG/ZIP, synced `/Applications/Donby.app` in-place, and published GitHub Pre-release `v2.1.3` (triggering `macos-15-intel` CI for `x86_64`).

## v2.1.2 | 2026-11-27 | Pre-release | Hide Top-Right Network Speed for Offline/Local Playback & Scope to Active Video Wire Speed
- **Scope**: `DonbyCore` (`ResolvedPlaybackResource`), `DonbyApp` (`PlayerView`, `PlayerView+HUD`, `PlayerView+PlaybackEngine`, `PlayerView+Drawers`), `DonbyCoreTests`, `build_app.sh`, `scripts/package_dmg.sh`, `docs/agent`
- **Root Cause / Goal**: Prevent the player HUD top-right speed indicator (and Media Info drawer speed row) from displaying local disk read throughput when playing offline downloads or local files, and keep the displayed rate scoped exclusively to the active video's network wire speed.
- **Changes**:
  - `Sources/DonbyCore/ResolvedPlaybackResource.swift` (`isLocalOrOfflineMedia`): Added canonical detection for `offline-` sessions, `file://` URLs, and bare POSIX local file paths without misclassifying `http`/`https` streams.

  - `Sources/DonbyApp/Player/PlayerView+PlaybackEngine.swift`, `PlayerView.swift`: Forced `displayDownloadSpeedMBs = 0.0` for offline/local playback instead of falling back to kernel AVIO read speed (`freshStats.download_speed_mbs`), and kept online playback synced to `activeTurboHandle.currentWireSpeedMBs` during opening/progress ticks and control-bar reveal.
  - `Tests/DonbyCoreTests/DonbyCoreTests.swift` (`resolvedPlaybackResourceLocalOrOfflineDetection`): Added unit tests covering local file URLs, `offline-` sessions, bare POSIX paths, and remote HTTP/HTTPS streams.
- **Invariants & Pitfalls**:

- **Verification**:

## v2.1.1 | 2026-11-27 | Pre-release | Architecture 2A: Zero-Crate Rust Media Data Plane, 8-Lane BDP Scheduler & Sparse Direct-Write Store
- **Scope**: `KernelRust` (`net/{mod,slab,disk_store,disk_reconcile,avio_bridge}`, `c_api_net`, `player`, `lifecycle`, `lib`), `DonbyKernelC` (`donby_kernel.h`), `DonbyNetwork` (`TieredAssetChunkStore*`, `TurboStreamingSessionHub`, `TurboStreamByteHandle*`, `DownloadStreamingHelpers`, `DonbyDownloadManager*`), `DonbyApp` (`Player/PlayerView+PlaybackEngine`), `DonbyCore` (`AppUpdatePolicies`), `Package.swift`, `DonbyCoreTests`, `build_app.sh`, `scripts/package_dmg.sh`, `docs/agent`
- **Root Cause / Goal**: Eliminate Swift `Data` reallocation/copy churn, out-of-order `chunks/<idx>.chk` double-write amplification, the `4`-lane / `< 4.5 MB/s` HTTP/2 concurrency ceiling, and `4–6 MiB` steady-state Range TTFB sawtooth bubbles while preserving `URLSession` macOS system proxy/PAC/HTTP3 compatibility and zero Cargo dependencies ([ADR-005](../decisions/005-zero-crate-rust-media-data-plane-2a.md)).
- **Changes**:
  - **Zero-Dependency `KernelRust` Media Data Core (`KernelRust/src/net/*`, `c_api_net.rs`, `donby_kernel.h`)**: Added physical-RAM-tiered `GlobalSlabPool` (`96/160/256/384 MiB` fixed `512 KiB` pages with head/tail anchor pinning), `DKChunkStore` full APFS sparse `media.bin` POSIX `pread`/`pwrite` storage + FNV-64 `chunks.bitmap` + `SEEK_DATA`/`SEEK_HOLE` reconciliation + legacy `.chk` one-shot folding + tombstone `invalidate_for_removal()`, `DKStreamBridge` in-kernel `AVIO` fast path (`dk_player_attach_stream_bridge`), `DKDownloadAssembler`, and `PlaybackCompanionPacer`.
  - **Adaptive `4..8` Lane Hub & `8–32 MiB` BDP Range Scheduler (`TurboStreamingSessionHub.swift`, `TurboStreamByteHandle*.swift`, `TieredAssetChunkStore*.swift`, `PlayerView+PlaybackEngine.swift`)**: Expanded `TurboStreamingSessionHub` to `4` warm + `4` on-demand lanes (`maxStreamLaneCount = 8`) with `withUnsafeBytes` zero-copy partial slab ingestion, removed the `< 4.5 MB/s` H2 boost lock in favor of bitrate-vs-cushion scaling, expanded steady-state Range spans to `8 / 16 / 32 MiB` once `leadCachedChunks >= 16`, and wired `PlayerView` to `dk_player_attach_stream_bridge`.
  - **Delegate-Direct Offline Downloader & Companion Pacer (`DownloadStreamingHelpers.swift`, `DonbyDownloadManager*.swift`)**: Replaced `AsyncThrowingStream` + `Data.removeSubrange` worker buffering with `openDirectStream` + `DKDownloadAssembler` direct `SlabPage` commits, and enabled token-bucket-paced single-worker companion downloading when active playback holds `>= 64` chunks (`32 MiB`) of steady cushion.
- **Invariants & Pitfalls**:
  - Keep `KernelRust/Cargo.toml` `[dependencies]` empty (`0` crates); never invoke Swift C callbacks while holding any Rust mutex (`DKStreamBridge::partials_mtx -> DKChunkStore::meta_mtx -> GlobalSlabPool::pool_mtx`); never open `media.bin` with `O_CREAT` on read paths; keep `DONBY_PURE=1` completely free of `DonbyUHDModule`.
- **Verification**:
  - `./scripts/dev.sh test` & `DONBY_PURE=1 ./scripts/dev.sh test`: Rust kernel (`13/13`), Swift `DonbyCoreTests` (`63/63` full / `58/58` pure), and `check_agent_docs.py` (`176` owned files, `<= 800L`, `0` orphans, `0` broken links) PASS.
  - `./scripts/release_local.sh 2.1.1 --prerelease`: packaged, synced `/Applications/Donby.app` in-place, and published GitHub Pre-release `v2.1.1`.
