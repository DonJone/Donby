# Changelog Shard: v2.0.x

## v2.0.25 | 2026-11-27 | Pre-release | Turbo Engine v3: Zero-Serial-RTT Instant Play, Tail-First Cues, Non-Blocking Kernel Seek & Micro-Hedge
- **Scope**: `DonbyNetwork` (`NetworkRouteWarmStore`, `TurboStreamingSessionHub*`, `TurboStreamByteHandle*`, `TurboCacheAndDownloadEngine`, `TurboCacheEngine+Preload`, `PlaybackIntentCoordinator`, `EmbyClient*`), `KernelRust` (`player.rs`, `lifecycle.rs`, `demux.rs`, `audio.rs`), `DonbyApp` (`AppState*`, `DetailView*`, `Player/PlayerView*`), `DonbyUHDModule` (`UHDModuleConfig`, `UHDStreamClient*`), `DonbyCore` (`MediaServiceProvider`, `AppUpdatePolicies`), `DonbyCoreTests` (`InstantPlayArchitectureTests`, `NetworkPlaybackAndDownloadTests`, `DonbyCoreTests`), `build_app.sh`, `scripts/package_dmg.sh`, `docs/agent`
- **Root Cause / Goal**: Eliminate the 6 remaining multi-RTT latency bottlenecks observed in `v2.0.24` 4K 65-track MKV Remux logs: (1) `350-600ms` pre-player control-plane wait and missing movie/hover/next-episode staging; (2) `1.35-1.98s` cold open Cues inversion caused by `coveredByInFlight` blocking exact-offset Cues reads behind a `2 MiB` sequential tail crawl; (3) `753-4053ms` post-open synchronous `dk_shim_seek_format` blackout before `markInitialOpenCompleted()` and CoreAudio HAL init; (4) cold 4-lane TLS/H2 handshakes and blind round-robin lane selection; (5) single-click seek `0.55s` 2-stream throttling and `1387ms` slow-lane head-of-line `rebuf=1` (`wire=6.08 MB/s`); (6) in-memory-only protocol/edge/302 route state across restarts.
- **Changes**:
  - **Persistent Route Warm Store & Telemetry-Scored 4-Lane Hub (`NetworkRouteWarmStore.swift`, `TurboStreamingSessionHub.swift`, `TurboStreamingSessionHub+Prewarm.swift`, `EmbyClient.swift`, `UHDStreamClient.swift`)**: Added `DONBY_HOME`-isolated `0600` persistence for HTTP/2-3 host capabilities (`7d`), resolved edge origins (`300s`), and `302` direct URLs (`1800s`, auto-invalidated on `403/404/410`), per-lane `ewmaTTFB`/`ewmaThroughputBps`/`warmHosts` scoring, zombie throttled-lane recycling (`< 25%` peer median & `< 450 KB/s`), and zero-byte 4-lane `HEAD` TLS/H2 prewarming (`DonbyNetworkSession.prewarmEdgeOrigin`).
  - **1-RTT Open Fan-Out, Tail-First Cues Bypass & Blocked-Reader Micro-Hedge (`TurboStreamByteHandle.swift`, `TurboStreamByteHandle+Scheduler.swift`, `TurboStreamByteHandle+Completion.swift`)**: Converged cold `Chunk 0` probe to `512 KiB`, narrowed initial tail sprint to exact final `512 KiB` with `> 192 KiB` exact-offset bypass in `readDirectTailRange`, fanned out `Chunk 1..2` + `EOF 512 KiB` + `Resume Anchor 1.5 MiB` at `T=0` or immediately on `302` `Location` arrival (`handleEarlyRedirectResolved`), separated single-click seek (`2`-chunk stripes across warm lanes) from rapid scrub (`>= 2` streaks -> `0.35s` 2-stream gate), and added slow-lane tail-trim + `300ms` blocked-reader `512 KiB` cross-lane micro-hedge (preserving ADR-002's `>= 2.2s` pre-header floor when no verified fast lane exists).
  - **Intent-Aware Pre-Provisioning & Preload Split (`PlaybackIntentCoordinator.swift`, `TurboCacheEngine+Preload.swift`, `TurboCacheAndDownloadEngine.swift`, `MediaServiceProvider.swift`, `UHDModuleConfig.swift`)**: Extracted preload logic into `TurboCacheEngine+Preload.swift` with `1.5-2.5 MiB` `startMicroStagePreload`, parallelized UHD domain + movie/episode `assets` prewarm, and added `PlaybackIntentCoordinator` with a `0ms` synchronous `ResolvedPlaybackResource` fast table, launch/line-switch edge preconnect, Home top-2 `继续观看` control-plane warmup, and `180ms`-debounced hover/focus staging.
  - **Non-Blocking Kernel Initial Seek & Adaptive Audio Preroll (`KernelRust/src/{player,lifecycle,demux,audio}.rs`)**: Fast-forwarded near-zero resumes (`0.05 < start_sec <= 2.5`) locally from cached `Chunk 0` without remote `Cues` seeks, deferred `start_sec > 2.5` container seeks onto `demux_worker` (`initial_open_seek_pending`) so `open_with_tracks` returns in `0ms` in parallel with `markInitialOpenCompleted()` and CoreAudio `init_audio_queue`, and lowered initial network `AudioQueue` preroll to `10_240` samples (`213ms`) when `>= 1.5s` of forward packets are already queued.
  - **Optimistic Launch, Hover Staging & Seamless Episode Cut (`AppState*`, `DetailView*`, `Player/PlayerView*`)**: Extracted `PlayerView+TurboSetup.swift`, wired `0ms` fast-table hits in `AppState.startPlayback` and synchronous `PlayerView.switchToEpisode`, compressed `DetailView` control-plane pre-resolve to `50ms` + `220ms` chunk stage, added `.onHover`/focus episode micro-staging, and upgraded near-end next-episode prefetch (`>= 72%`, buffer `>= 12s`) to micro-stage head, Cues, and auto-intro-skip landing chunks.
- **Invariants & Pitfalls**:
  - Preserve all ADR-002 guarantees (never lock `h2` concurrency to `2` on `leadCachedChunks < 2`; keep `>= 2.2s` pre-header cold rescue floor whenever `hasVerifiedFastAlternateLane` is false; keep frozen 38-function `donby_kernel.h` C ABI untouched; keep `DONBY_PURE=1` completely free of `DonbyUHDModule` references).
- **Verification**:
  - `./scripts/dev.sh test` & `DONBY_PURE=1 ./scripts/dev.sh test`: Rust kernel (`8/8`), Swift `DonbyCoreTests` (6 suites including 5 new loopback socket simulation tests in `InstantPlayArchitectureTests`), and `check_agent_docs.py` (`<= 800L`, `1:1` inventory) PASS.
  - `./scripts/release_local.sh 2.0.25 --prerelease`: packaged, synced `/Applications/Donby.app` in-place, and published GitHub Pre-release `v2.0.25`.

## v2.0.24 | 2026-11-27 | Pre-release | Multi-Lane Streaming Pool, Instant-Start Parallel Prefetch & Playback-Priority Download Yielding
- **Scope**: `DonbyNetwork` (`TurboStreamingSessionHub`, `DownloadStreamingHelpers`, `TurboStreamByteHandle*`, `TurboCacheAndDownloadEngine`, `DonbyDownloadManager*`, `TieredAssetChunkStore`), `DonbyApp` (`Player/PlayerView*`, `DetailView+DataLoading`, `AppState+DownloadsAndCache`), `DonbyCore` (`AppUpdatePolicies`), `DonbyCoreTests` (`DonbyCoreTests`, `NetworkPlaybackAndDownloadTests`), `build_app.sh`, `scripts/package_dmg.sh`, `docs/agent`
- **Root Cause / Goal**: Runtime logs showed `wire=0.29-0.59 MB/s` mid-playback stalls, `1.15s` Cues cold-read waits on 64-track 4K MKVs (`chunk=18636`), and serial RTT overhead on cold resume opens because: (1) `TurboStreamingSessionHub` multiplexed all Turbo playback streams and Emby/UHD API calls onto one `URLSession` (single HTTP/2 TCP flow); (2) `startDetailPreload` used throwaway ephemeral sessions and fetched head/tail/runway serially; (3) `> 4 GiB` container tail probes prefetched only `1 MiB` and cold resume did not speculatively warm the resume anchor; (4) `DonbyDownloadManager` ran `maxConcurrentDownloads = 2` concurrently with active playback, lacked a Worker-0 302 resolution barrier (causing 6-worker origin redirect bursts), lacked cross-host `Authorization` stripping on redirects, and used mismatched 6s-vs-2.5s crawl windows.
- **Changes**:
  - **4-Lane Multi-TCP Streaming Hub & API Isolation (`TurboStreamingSessionHub.swift`, `TurboStreamByteHandle+Completion.swift`)**: Separated `DonbyNetworkSession.sharedURLSession` into an isolated `apiURLSession` and upgraded `TurboStreamingSessionHub` to 4 `ephemeral` streaming lanes (independent TCP/TLS flows with strictly serial `delegateQueue`), adding least-loaded scheduling, cross-lane rescue (`excludeLane`), per-lane stall recycling (`reportLaneStall`/`reportLaneHealthy`), cross-host `Authorization` stripping on redirects, and immediate slow-primary cancellation when a rescue attempt wins.
  - **Instant-Start Tail/Resume Prefetch & Warm-Lane Detail Preload (`TurboStreamByteHandle.swift`, `TurboStreamByteHandle+Scheduler.swift`, `TurboCacheAndDownloadEngine.swift`, `DetailView+DataLoading.swift`, `PlayerView+PlaybackEngine.swift`)**: Expanded `> 4 GiB` EOF Cues window to `2 MiB` (`4` chunks), added parallel speculative resume-anchor prefetch (`initialStartSeconds > 5`, `budget >= 3`), and upgraded `startDetailPreload` to reuse `warmStreamSession(forLane:)` with parallel Header + Cues + `4 MiB` playhead runway warm-up.
  - **Playback-Priority Download Yielding & Worker Coordination (`DonbyDownloadManager.swift`, `DownloadStreamingHelpers.swift`, `DonbyDownloadManager+WorkerAndMigration.swift`, `DonbyDownloadManager+Migration.swift`, `TieredAssetChunkStore.swift`, `AppState+DownloadsAndCache.swift`)**: Set `maxConcurrentDownloads = 1` (fairly splitting budget if raised), added nested `beginPlaybackNetworkPriority`/`endPlaybackNetworkPriority` yielding during remote playback, added Worker-0 initial 302 barrier, early redirect callback + cross-host `Authorization` stripping in `DownloadRangeStreamDelegate`, `ActiveWorkerCounter` + `3.5s` aligned crawl window, degraded-CDN re-resolution after 2 stall recycles, progress-aware retry accounting, and relaxed disk backpressure (`128`/`96`).
- **Invariants & Pitfalls**:
  - Keep `URLSession` `delegateQueue.maxConcurrentOperationCount == 1` on every streaming lane and download worker; keep `beginPlaybackNetworkPriority` paired with `endPlaybackNetworkPriority` on error/stop paths; never bypass `<= 800L` file caps (extracted `DownloadStreamingHelpers.swift`, `TurboStreamByteHandle+Completion.swift`, `DonbyDownloadManager+Migration.swift`).
- **Verification**:
  - `./scripts/dev.sh test`: Rust kernel (`8/8`), Swift `DonbyCoreTests` (`56/56` across 5 suites including `NetworkPlaybackAndDownloadTests`), and `check_agent_docs.py` (`164` owned files, `0` orphans, `0` broken links) PASS.
  - `DONBY_PURE=1 ./scripts/dev.sh build`: PASS.
  - `./scripts/release_local.sh 2.0.24 --prerelease`: packaged, synced `/Applications/Donby.app` in-place, and published GitHub Pre-release `v2.0.24`.
